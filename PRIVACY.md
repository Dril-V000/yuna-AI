# YUNA Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 26, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Code-7** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit the YUNA website, download or use the YUNA desktop application ("YUNA" or the "Software"), activate a license, or contact us.

YUNA is designed to let you run AI locally on your own Windows device. The information processed depends on the features and services you choose to use.

## 1. Information We Process

### 1.1 License and Device-Security Information

When you activate or verify a YUNA license, the Software communicates with our licensing service. To validate the license and protect it against unauthorized use, we may process:

- Your YUNA license key;
- A one-way cryptographic hash derived from a hardware identifier (HWID), rather than the original hardware identifier in plain text;
- An encrypted/protected stored representation of that derived hash;
- The IP address associated with the activation or verification request;
- Activation, verification, timestamp, and security-event records.

The HWID-derived hash and the associated IP address are stored by our licensing service for license validation, device binding, fraud prevention, and investigation of suspected unauthorized access or license sharing. The HWID-derived value is stored in protected, encrypted form; it is not displayed publicly or made available to other users. Access to license records is restricted to authorized personnel and systems for legitimate operational and security purposes.

The original HWID is not intended to be transmitted or stored as plain text by the licensing service. The hash is used to recognize the licensed device without needing to keep the original hardware identifier.

### 1.2 Support and Communications

If you contact us, we may process the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, Discord username, license key, purchase reference, device or error details, screenshots, and the contents of your message. We use this information to respond to you, provide support, investigate issues, and protect our services.

Please do not send passwords, payment-card security codes, wallet recovery phrases, or private keys. We do not need them to provide support or verify a purchase.

### 1.3 Website and Technical Logs

When you visit our website or use online licensing features, the relevant hosting or service infrastructure may process technical information such as IP addresses, request timestamps, browser or device information, and error or security logs. These records help operate, maintain, and secure the website and licensing service.

## 2. Information Stored Locally by YUNA

When you use local features, your conversations, memory, settings, prompts, and files may be stored on your own device.

- These local files are not automatically uploaded to Code-7's licensing service.
- Code-7 does not have direct access to your locally stored conversations or files through normal local operation of YUNA.
- Local data may be accessible to people or software that have access to your Windows account or device. Unless a specific feature says otherwise, do not assume that local application files are separately encrypted.
- You can manage or delete local data using the controls provided by YUNA, where available, or by managing the relevant files on your device.

Your operating system, backups, antivirus software, or other applications may process local files independently of YUNA.

## 3. Third-Party AI Providers

YUNA can be used with local AI models. If you choose to connect an external AI provider or cloud API (which may include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Groq, or another provider supported by your configuration), the prompts and other information needed to fulfil your request may be sent to that provider.

Those requests are handled under the selected provider's own terms, privacy policy, and data practices. The provider may process or retain submitted information according to your account, settings, and its policies. Any provider usage fees are separate from the YUNA license.

Review the provider's privacy policy before sending personal, confidential, or sensitive information. Choosing a cloud provider is optional; local AI features can keep their AI processing on your device, subject to the behavior of the model and other services you independently configure.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information described in this Policy for the following purposes:

- Activating, validating, and managing YUNA licenses;
- Binding a license to its authorized device;
- Detecting and preventing license sharing, key theft, fraud, unauthorized access, and attempts to bypass licensing protections;
- Maintaining the reliability, security, and performance of our website and services;
- Responding to support requests and resolving technical problems;
- Meeting applicable legal obligations and protecting our legal rights.

We do not use local conversations or files stored on your device for advertising or licensing analytics.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent personal information.

We may share limited information only when reasonably necessary:
- With hosting, infrastructure, or service providers that operate the website or licensing service on our behalf;
- With professional advisers or authorities where required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security;
- In connection with investigating suspected fraud, unauthorized license use, or security incidents.

Service providers are permitted to process information only as needed to provide their services or meet applicable obligations. Information submitted directly to a third-party AI provider is governed by that provider's policies, as explained in Section 3.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

We retain license and security records for as long as reasonably necessary to operate license validation, maintain device binding, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Support communications and technical logs are retained for the period reasonably needed for their purposes.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we control by emailing **hello@yunaai.online** with the subject "YUNA Privacy Request". We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the request relates to you.

Some records may need to be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims or protect the licensing service. Deleting license/device records may prevent us from validating or restoring the associated activation.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed by our website and licensing service. For example, the HWID-derived hash is stored in protected, encrypted form and access to license records is restricted.

No online service, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

YUNA is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact **hello@yunaai.online** so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

## 9. International Processing

Our website, hosting, licensing infrastructure, and third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is handled with appropriate safeguards.
